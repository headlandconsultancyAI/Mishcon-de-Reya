            How this was measured

            • Source: LinkedIn posts from the profiles of __NSTAFF__ Mishcon de Reya partners, knowledge lawyers and business leaders, scraped weekly. This version covers a rolling 12 months, __START_LONG__ to __END_LONG__. Departments come from the people list in the repo.
            • __NPEOPLE__ people had at least one post or repost captured. The other __NSILENT__ are listed separately. Most will simply not have posted, but a few profiles couldn't be matched, so treat that list as indicative.
            • Only original posts count. A repost shows the original author's engagement, so including it would credit people for other posts' performance.
            • Median is the main measure because one viral post can drag an average a long way. The median shows what a typical post gets.
            • People with fewer than 5 original posts are shown separately. Their median rests on too few posts to compare fairly.
            • Engagement is as captured on the scrape date, so the newest posts may still be picking up reactions.
